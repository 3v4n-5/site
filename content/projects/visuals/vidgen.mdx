The video half of [Imgen](/projects/visuals/imgen): kept stills animated by Wan 2.2 image-to-video on a throwaway RunPod pod, driven from the same local CLI.

Generation runs in stages: hand-jotted notes → <RefPreview><RefContent /></RefPreview> → stills → <RefPreview label="prompts.md"><PromptsContent /></RefPreview> → video. The still supplies the scene; the motion prompt says only what moves.

## Pipeline

**1. Still.** A kept SDXL render from Imgen, e.g. `c003-A_xeno_high-s11453`.

**2. Still → video.** Wan 2.2 i2v 14B (fp8) renders 81 frames at 16 fps, 640²: one 5 s segment. The high-noise expert lays out the motion, the low-noise expert refines it. Lightning LoRAs cut the step count from 30 to 4–8.

**3. Sweep.** `imgen-sweep` runs a plan of still × motion specs on one pod: single segments, chains (each segment seeded from the previous last frame, colour-anchored to the base still), or hard cuts back to the base still. Resumable; `index.tsv` records still, motion prompt, workflow, seed and render time.

## Workflows

Motion scores are from one violent test prompt.

- **`l4`**: Lightning LoRAs at 1.0, 4 steps, CFG 1. Stable colour, motion 4.0. Current default.
- **`lightning`**: Lightning at 0.4, 8 steps. Stable colour, motion 2.2. Too tame.
- **`hybrid`, `hot`**: motion 4–6, but colour drifts to flat green within one segment.
- **No Lightning**: 30 steps, CFG 6. ~30 min per segment on a 4090.

 The clips below are from the first two sweeps, before `l4`: one `lightning`, four `hybrid`.

## Setup

- ComfyUI on RunPod (4090, then A6000, then L40S), driven headless from a local CLI
- One pod per invocation: create → watchdog → pull models (~38 GB, ~2.5 min) → generate → terminate. No network volume and no stopped pods, since both bill while idle
- On-pod watchdog terminates after 15 min idle, one job over 20 min, or a session over 90 min; every local exit path terminates too, and each command ends with an account check for stray pods
- Review in a local browser viewer: arrow keys move, Delete rejects, F favourites

## Review

From 314 favourited clips:
- Moving-but-not-violent clips beat intense ones.
- Intense prompts that name a creature arriving add one when the still has none.
- Prompts with fluid, spray or debris produce water bursting from nowhere.
- Hard cuts jump back to the opening frame at every cut. With `l4` and the colour anchor, intense clips chain instead.
- Low prompt variety: 6 intense prompts across ~150 clips spread the same faults everywhere. Now one prompt per output.
